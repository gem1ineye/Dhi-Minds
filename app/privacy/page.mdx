<LegalLayout title="Privacy Policy" lastUpdated="8 August 2026">

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data **Dhi Minds** ("we", "us") collects
through this website, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how to request access,
correction, or deletion.

## 1. What we collect

The only personal data this website actively collects is what you submit through the project
inquiry form: name, email address, company (optional), phone number (optional), the service and
budget range you select, your project description, your timeline, and your consent status. We
also record technical metadata with each submission — a hashed (not raw) IP address, timestamp,
and any UTM/referrer parameters — for spam prevention and marketing attribution.

We do not use cookies for advertising, and analytics does not receive any personal data or form
content (see §5).

## 2. Why we collect it

We process this data to respond to your inquiry, scope a potential project, and — if you agree —
follow up about work with Dhi Minds. The lawful basis is your consent, given explicitly
via the checkbox on the inquiry form, and our legitimate interest in responding to business
inquiries sent to us directly.

## 3. Where it is stored

Inquiry data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database. Email notifications generated by a submission
are processed by our transactional email provider. We do not sell or rent personal data to third
parties.

## 4. How long we keep it

Inquiry records are retained for <strong>{siteConfig.retentionPeriod}</strong> from the date of
submission, after which they are deleted or anonymised by a scheduled process, unless a longer
period is required to comply with a legal obligation or an active engagement.

## 5. Who else processes it

Two categories of processor may handle data in the course of operating this site:

- **Email delivery** — used to send the team notification and, where enabled, your auto-reply.
- **Hosting and analytics** — used to serve the site and, if analytics is enabled, to understand
  aggregate traffic. Analytics never receives form field values or other personal data (see the
  site's `lib/analytics` implementation).

## 6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent,
at any time by contacting us at <a href={`mailto:${siteConfig.grievanceContact}`}>{siteConfig.grievanceContact}</a>.
This is also our designated grievance contact under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
2023. If you are located in the EU/EEA or California, the same contact route is used to honour
GDPR or CCPA requests (access, correction, deletion, and opt-out).

## 7. Security

Submissions are transmitted over HTTPS, validated and sanitised on the server, and access to the
underlying database is restricted. No system is completely immune to risk, but we take reasonable,
industry-standard measures to protect what you share with us.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our processors change. The "Last updated" date at the top
of this page reflects the most recent revision.
